Privacy & your data.

The game works without registering an account. Game scores, streaks and your preferred language are stored only in your browser's local storage. You can clear them using your browser's site data controls. There are no advertising cookies, email sign-ups, personalized advertisements or analytics pixels in this version.

For website delivery, Surge static hosting may process technical connection information under its own privacy policy. If you visit the game inside Telegram, the Telegram client may pass technical user context to the Mini App; this public demo does not upload Telegram identity information to any server.

Game sharing is initiated only when you press a share button. No contacts are read and no unsolicited messages are sent. Paid features and global rankings are not active.

Support and privacy requests: use the contact details published in the legal notice. See also the public hosting provider at surge.sh.

If the game is upgraded to a bot-backed service, this policy will be updated to detail the purpose, retention, and deletion of server-side gameplay records before accounts are activated.

Last updated 30 October 2026.
